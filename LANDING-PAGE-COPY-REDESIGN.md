## 📱 MOBILE-FIRST CONSIDERATIONS

**Hero Headlines (Mobile)**
- Keep under 8 words
- One clear benefit
- Example: "Stop Chasing Contractors. Start Building."

**Bullet Points**
- Max 10 words per bullet
- Lead with the benefit
- End with the feature

**CTAs (Mobile)**
- Finger-friendly sizing (44px minimum)
- One primary CTA per screen
- Use contrasting colors

---

## 🎨 TONE GUIDELINES

### **Professional Yet Approachable**
- Speak like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson
- Use "you" and "your" (not "users" or "clients")
- Be direct, not corporate

### **Authoritative**
- Make definitive statements
- Use data when possible ("80% faster")
- Show expertise through specificity

### **Innovative**
- Position as the modern solution
- Contrast with old ways ("stop chasing", "no more chaos")
- Use forward-looking language
